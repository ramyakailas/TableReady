Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

TableReady is built to keep your meal planning private. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

Nothing. TableReady does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account creation, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — menus, dishes, your grocery list, and family member names — is stored locally on your own device, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to a server we operate, because we don't operate one.

Syncing between devices

TableReady does not sync data between your iPhone and Mac. Each device keeps its own independent copy of your data.

Third parties

TableReady does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services.

Children's privacy

TableReady does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
