Common questions

  • Does TableReady sync my data? Your menus, dishes, and grocery list are stored on your device. iPhone and Mac each keep their own copy.
  • Can I import a whole week of menus at once? Yes — use "Import Menus" from the Menu tab and paste a menu set.
  • How is a menu's effort level (light/medium/heavy) decided? It's calculated automatically from bread count and whether the menu involves deep frying.
